Paintings –
Color as a Living Force

My paintings grow from layered textures and organic structures — acrylic, ink, and watercolor applied until something unexpected appears. Each work balances expressive gesture with quiet intention — atmosphere and depth finding their own way to the surface. Rooted in contemporary abstract art, these textured artworks bring a distinctive presence to modern interiors while inviting a closer encounter with color, form, and process.

Paintings — written by water

My paintings are abstract, yet never without memory. Standing before a blank sheet of paper, I am never quite sure whether I am beginning something or returning to something long known — and it is within that uncertainty that the first gesture appears.

I have always loved paper. It receives everything: it lets watercolor bloom and wander, carries the density of acrylic, draws ink deep into its fibers, and holds the soft breath of pastel. Each material asks for a different patience, and I follow where it leads.

Color, for me, is not decoration but temperature and direction — yellows that warm, blues that draw inward, reds that refuse silence. I layer them slowly, until what remains is not the image of a place but the trace of an encounter: a meeting of intention and chance, where a hidden poetic geography of the inner world rises quietly to the surface.
