Cyanotype - The Alchemy of Sun and Blue

Enter the world of my original cyanotype artworks, created through experimental processes beneath the sun of Tenerife. Using plants, leaves, and organic textures, these contemporary cyanotypes combine traditional photographic printing techniques with modern mixed-media experimentation, revealing luminous compositions in deep blue tones.
