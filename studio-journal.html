Agenda Artística Astrológica 2027

Dear friends,

My Agenda Artística Astrológica 2027 is now finished — a bilingual art calendar in Spanish and German, created to accompany you throughout the coming year.

It features 14 full-color reproductions of cyanotypes I created under the sun of Tenerife. Each month begins with a lunar calendar showing the new and full moons, as well as the Moon's passage through the signs of the zodiac. There is plenty of space throughout the calendar for writing, drawing, or simply doodling. On some pages, you'll also find a selection of texts and thoughts that have accompanied me over the past year.

The calendar is available in DIN A5 and DIN A6.

Since I will be having the calendars printed in advance and the edition will be limited, I need to receive firm orders by the deadline. Unfortunately, there will be no reprint. If you would like a calendar, please send me your firm order by the end of October 2026, indicating which format you prefer (DIN A5 or DIN A6).

I'll be happy to send you the price and further information upon request.

I hope you enjoy the agenda and that it will accompany you, even if only for a little while, throughout 2027.

Creative ARTWORK / News from my studio

Fragment of a Memory

My latest work – a fired ceramic piece on a column-like base – a unique piece – a fragment of a memory of mine that I thought had long been forgotten.

Like a relic unearthed from an inner landscape, this work bears traces of a forgotten language. Its ceramic surface reveals signs, impressions and traces that seem to hover between memory and imagination. Resting on a dark metal column, the fragment becomes a vessel for that which persists beneath conscious memory. It is not the memory itself that returns, but its residue – altered by time, yet still capable of speaking through texture, form and material presence.

Reflection / Essay

The Magic of Totems

A totem is an archetypal symbol expressing the profound connection between human beings and nature—manifested through animals, plants, mountains, rivers, or springs. It reflects an awareness of our belonging to the natural world, rooted in the collective unconscious and the transpersonal dimension of human experience.

In today’s technologically mediated world, this awareness is increasingly fading, and with it an essential source of spiritual depth and individual creativity.

As symbolic forms, totems mediate between the collective unconscious and individual consciousness. Across cultures, this understanding has been preserved through myth, ritual, and totemic imagery.

In my artistic practice, the motif of the totem appears as a recurring symbolic structure. Through paintings and my Totem sculptures, I explore the relationship between archaic memory, inner experience, and the spiritual dimensions of nature. My work evokes universal archetypal energies and seeks to make the primal and the transpersonal tangible through contemporary art.

Review

artbook Berlin 2025

In November 2025, I took part in artbook.berlin, the annual fair for artist books and small publishers at Kunstquartier Bethanien in Berlin-Kreuzberg. Over three days, I presented a selection of my work alongside book artists, printmakers, and independent publishers from Berlin and beyond.

It was a wonderful opportunity to meet other artists and present my work to a new audience in the city that I now call home. I would like to extend my special thanks to Cornelius and Hanneke for their excellent organization and warm hospitality.
