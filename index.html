I am a multidisciplinary artist based in Berlin — working across abstract painting, sculpture, and cyanotype.

For me, art is a bridge between my inner world and the outside world. My work carries a visual language shaped by Caribbean, indigenous, and Latin American influences — forms that emerged long before Berlin, and continue here.

Through my work, I explore the tension between organic forms and human perception. Each piece becomes a dialogue between material and time — between what appears, fades, and quietly remains.

Each piece asks the same question, just in a different language — and the bridge is always art.
