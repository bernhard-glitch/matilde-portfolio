Art as a Dialogue

I am a multidisciplinary artist based in Berlin. My work moves through the spaces between abstract painting, sculpture, and experimental printmaking techniques such as cyanotype.

Through my work, I explore the tension between organic forms and human perception. Each piece becomes a dialogue between material and time — between what appears, fades, and quietly remains.

I live by the belief that art is not separate from life, but part of its rhythm and breath. "I live by art" is more than a credo for me; it is the thread that runs through my entire artistic practice.
